Where your money goes

100% of the funds raised through this project are dedicated to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (OSMF). Your donation directly supports:

  • Server infrastructure and bandwidth costs.
  • Development of core mapping tools.
  • Legal protection for the open data license.
  • Community events and mapping parties.

🇺🇦 The Heart of Leaflet is in Ukraine

The mapping library powering this project, Leaflet, was created in 2010 by Volodymyr Agafonkin, a Ukrainian citizen living in Kyiv. Since the full-scale Russian invasion in 2022, the project has been vocal about the war's impact on its creator and community.

Volodymyr and the Leaflet community strongly recommend supporting the "Come Back Alive" charity (Povernys Zhyvym). This organization is critical in supplying technology, training, and equipment to the Armed Forces of Ukraine to save lives on the front lines.

"If an appeal to humanity doesn't work for you, I'll appeal to your egoism: the future of Ukrainian citizens is the future of Leaflet." — Volodymyr Agafonkin
