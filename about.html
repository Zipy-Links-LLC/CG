About the 49th Parallel Project

This interactive map visualization explores the geopolitical and geographical significance of the 49th Parallel North. Best known as the primary border between the United States and Canada from the Lake of the Woods to the Strait of Georgia, this line of latitude circles the entire globe.

49° N Latitude
~180° Longitude Span
Global Scope

Project Purpose

The goal of this tool is to allow users to visualize territories, calculate land areas, and creatively define regions strictly below this historic boundary line. Whether for educational purposes, alternate history scenarios, or urban planning concepts, the map provides a clean canvas constrained by the 49th parallel.

Technical Details

Built using Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap data, this application runs entirely in your browser. No data is stored on a server; all drawings and calculations are performed locally in your session.
